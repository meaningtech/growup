# Security policy

## Reporting a vulnerability

Do not open a public issue for a suspected vulnerability or exposed credential. Email `hello@meaningtech.io` with:

- the affected component or endpoint;
- reproduction steps or a proof of concept;
- the likely impact;
- any suggested mitigation.

Please avoid accessing, changing or retaining data that does not belong to you. We will acknowledge the report, investigate it and coordinate disclosure when appropriate.

## Supported version

Security fixes target the current `main` branch and the production service at [growup.earth](https://growup.earth).
